About PSPH

Public School Pension Help (PSPH) helps public school employees understand their pensions by matching them with pension professionals for a free, one-on-one session.

Why PSPH exists

Pensions are one of the most valuable benefits school employees have, and one of the hardest to understand. Plan rules, tiers, service credit, and Social Security interactions are complicated, and most people never get a chance to ask questions about their own situation. PSPH makes that conversation easy to get.

Who we serve

Public school employees in all 50 states: teachers, paraprofessionals, administrators, support staff, and higher-education employees, at any stage of their career.

What a session looks like

  • A free one-hour Zoom meeting with a pension specialist
  • Built around your questions about your pension, retirement timing, Social Security, and supplemental savings
  • A summary email afterward

How PSPH is funded

Sessions are free to you. PSPH matches public school employees with pension professionals, and the firms that employ those professionals pay PSPH. You never pay PSPH anything.

What PSPH is not

  • PSPH does not sell financial products.
  • PSPH is not affiliated with any school district, union, or pension system.
  • PSPH does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice. Your pension system is the official source for your benefits.

Questions about your pension?

Book a free session, or email us at hello@psph.org. We reply within 1 business day.
