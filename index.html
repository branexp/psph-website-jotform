Frequently Asked Questions

What will we cover in a session?

Your pension benefit and how it's calculated, your retirement timing options, how Social Security fits in, and any supplemental accounts such as a 403(b) or 457(b). Bring your questions; the session is built around them.

Is there a cost? How long is the session?

Sessions are free. Each one is a one-hour Zoom meeting with a pension specialist.

How is PSPH paid if sessions are free?

PSPH matches public school employees with pension professionals. The firms that employ those professionals pay PSPH. You never pay PSPH anything.

Does PSPH sell financial products?

No. PSPH does not sell financial products. Our role is to connect you with a specialist who can explain your pension in plain language.

Who can book a session?

Public school employees in all 50 states, including teachers, paraprofessionals, administrators, support staff, and higher-education employees.

Do I need to be close to retirement?

No. Early- and mid-career employees often get the most out of a session, because decisions about service credit and supplemental savings have more time to make a difference.

What should I have ready?

If you can, your most recent pension statement or an estimate of your years of service, and a recent pay stub. It's fine to come with questions only.

Are you part of my pension system, district, or union?

No. PSPH is not affiliated with any school district, union, or pension system. Your pension system remains the official source for your benefits.
